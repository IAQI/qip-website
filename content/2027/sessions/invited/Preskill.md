## Biography

John Preskill is the Richard P. Feynman Professor of Theoretical Physics at the California Institute of Technology, where he holds the Allen V. C. Davis and Lenabelle Davis Leadership Chair and directs the Institute for Quantum Information and Matter (IQIM). Born in 1953, he received his A.B. from Princeton in 1975 and his Ph.D. from Harvard in 1980, and joined the Caltech faculty in 1983 after serving as a Junior Fellow and Associate Professor at Harvard. He began his career in particle physics and cosmology before turning to quantum information science. In 2000 he founded Caltech's Institute for Quantum Information, which became part of IQIM in 2011. His lecture notes on quantum computation, available online since 1997, have shaped the field. He coined the term "quantum supremacy" (now more often called quantum advantage) and introduced the notion of noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) devices. He is a member of the National Academy of Sciences and a fellow of the American Physical Society. His honours include the 2024 John Stewart Bell Prize and the 2025 Academic Pioneer in Quantum Award. He has mentored more than 60 Ph.D. students and more than 60 postdoctoral scholars.

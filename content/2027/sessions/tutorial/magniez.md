## Biography

Frédéric Magniez is a CNRS researcher at IRIF, Université Paris Cité. His research lies at the interface of randomized and quantum algorithms and complexity theory. In quantum computing, he contributed to the development of quantum-walk search algorithms and made early contributions to quantum self-testing, the hidden subgroup problem, and frameworks for proving quantum query lower bounds. More recently, he co-initiated the use of recording-query techniques to establish quantum time-space tradeoffs

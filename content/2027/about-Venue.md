The University Cultural Centre (UCC), located on the Kent Ridge campus of the National University of Singapore (NUS), is a premier performing arts venue. Officially opened in 2000, it is a cultural cornerstone for both the university and the broader public, renowned for its architectural design and top-tier acoustics. Ho Bee Auditorium main hall can accommodate up to 1,700 attendees. It features horseshoe-shaped seating with two tiers of balconies and is equipped with state-of-the-art immersive audio technology.

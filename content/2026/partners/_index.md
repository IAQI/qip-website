## Organized by

QIP 2026 is organized by the University of Latvia and the Latvian Quantum Initiative.

## Call for Sponsors

The annual QIP conference is the largest international event dedicated to the latest developments in quantum information processing. Researchers from all over the world present their recent scientific discoveries and companies display their latest relevant technology. The conference covers all aspects of quantum computing, cryptography, information theory, mathematics, and physics.

The 29th edition of the conference, QIP 2026, will take place at the ATTA Centre in Riga, Latvia on January 24-30, 2026. The conference is expected to host approximately 1,000 researchers and scientists from leading international academic research groups and engineers and industry representatives from international companies.

## What is QIP 2026?

QIP 2026 is the 29th Annual Quantum Information Processing Conference. Quantum Information Processing (QIP) is the largest quantum information technology conference, which annually gathers the world's brightest researchers and industry leaders to present and discuss the latest achievements in quantum computing, cryptography, information theory, mathematics and physics.

See the list of previous conferences <a style="color: yellow" href="/2026/charter/#history-of-qip">here</a>.

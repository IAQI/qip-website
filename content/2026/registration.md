# Registration

Registration for QIP 2026 is now closed.

QIP 2026 took place January 24-30, 2026 in Riga, Latvia. The conference brought together the world's leading researchers in quantum information processing for a week of talks, tutorials, and poster presentations.

- Tutorials (January 24-25, 2026)
- Main conference (January 26-30, 2026)

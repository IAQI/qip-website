# Travel & Accommodation

## Getting to Riga

### By Air

Riga International Airport (RIX) is the main airport serving the city, located approximately 10 km from the city center.

**From the Airport to the City:**
- **By Bus:** Bus 22 runs to the city center (approximately 30 minutes)
- **By Taxi:** Approximately 20-25 minutes to the city center or conference venues
- **By Rideshare:** Bolt and other services are available

### By Train

Riga Central Station (Rīgas Centrālā stacija) is located in the city center and connects to other Baltic and European cities.

### By Bus

International bus services connect Riga to other European cities. The main bus station is near the Central Railway Station.

---

## Accommodation

Riga offers a wide range of accommodation options from luxury hotels to budget-friendly hostels.

**Recommended Areas:**
- **Old Town (Vecrīga):** Historic center, walking distance to many attractions
- **City Center:** Close to public transportation and amenities
- **Near the Venues:** Consider proximity to ATTA Centre or House of Science

*Specific hotel recommendations and conference rates will be announced closer to the conference date.*

---

## Local Transportation

Riga has an efficient public transportation system including:
- **Trams**
- **Buses**
- **Trolleybuses**

Single tickets can be purchased on board or via the Rīgas Satiksme app. Consider a multi-day pass for the duration of the conference.

---

## Weather

Riga in late January typically experiences cold winter weather:
- **Average Temperature:** -3°C to -7°C (27°F to 19°F)
- **Conditions:** Possible snow, shorter daylight hours

We recommend bringing warm clothing, layers, and appropriate winter footwear.

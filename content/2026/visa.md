# Visa & Invitation Letters

## Visa Requirements

Latvia is a member of the European Union and the Schengen Area. Depending on your nationality, you may need a visa to enter Latvia.

**EU/EEA/Swiss Citizens:** No visa required.

**Other Nationalities:** Please check with your local Latvian embassy or consulate for visa requirements. You can also check the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Latvia](https://www.mfa.gov.lv/en) for the latest information.

---

## Invitation Letters

If you require an invitation letter for visa purposes, please register for the conference first and then contact the organizing committee at [qip2026@lu.lv](mailto:qip2026@lu.lv) with:

- Your full name (as it appears on your passport)
- Your passport number
- Your affiliation/institution
- Your registration confirmation number

**Please allow sufficient time for visa processing.** We recommend applying for your visa at least 2-3 months before the conference.

---

## Important Notes

- The invitation letter is provided solely to assist with visa applications and does not imply any financial commitment from the organizers.
- Conference registration and payment are required before an invitation letter can be issued.
- Please ensure all information provided matches your passport details exactly.

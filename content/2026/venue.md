# Conference Venues

QIP 2026 will be held at two venues in Riga, Latvia.

## Main Conference Venue: ATTA Centre

**Address:** Krasta iela 60, Riga, Latvia

The ATTA Centre is the largest and most modern conference venue in the Baltics, situated just 15 minutes drive from Riga Old Town.

**Getting There:**
- **By Tram:** Tram 7 (every 10 minutes) to "Rēznas iela" – 650m walk
- **By Bus:** Bus 12 (every 40 minutes) to "Balvu iela" – 600m walk
- **By Taxi:** 7–10 minutes from Riga Old Town, ~25 minutes from the airport

[View on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/RuHGNU3cmDqdXTW48)

## Tutorial Venue: University of Latvia, House of Science

**Address:** Jelgavas iela 3, Riga, Latvia

The House of Science is part of the University of Latvia's new campus, located near the city center and easily accessible by public transportation.

Tutorials will take place here on **January 24-25, 2026**.

**Getting There:**
- **By Bus:** Lines 3, 3a, 4, 7, 8, 10, 21, 22, 23, 25, 26, 32
- **By Tram:** Lines 1, 2, 5, 10
- **By Trolleybus:** Lines 9, 27 (stop "Nacionālā bibliotēka" – 700m walk)
- **Bus 23:** Stop "LU Akadēmiskais cents"
- **By Taxi:** ~7 minutes from Riga Old Town, ~20 minutes from the airport

[View on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/xE8RPtPf1pLhTNMj7)

## About Riga

Riga is the capital and largest city of Latvia, known for its Art Nouveau architecture, vibrant cultural scene, and well-preserved medieval Old Town (a UNESCO World Heritage Site).

The city offers excellent public transportation, diverse dining options, and a rich history spanning over 800 years.
